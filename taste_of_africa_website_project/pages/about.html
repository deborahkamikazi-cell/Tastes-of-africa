About us

Preserving Africa’s Culinary Heritage

Food is memory, identity and community. We exist to keep it alive.

Our mission

Celebrate. Preserve. Share.

Many African recipes live in the hands of grandmothers, never written down. Tastes of Africa documents them with respect so the next generation can cook, taste and remember.

A brief history

African cuisine spans millennia, from Nile-valley grains and Sahelian millets to spices carried along Indian Ocean and trans-Saharan trade routes.

🍛
🌍

Global influence

African ingredients and techniques shaped cooking from the Caribbean to the American South.

📜

Why it matters

Traditional food knowledge carries biodiversity, history and identity across generations.

🤝

Community

Recipes are contributed and verified by cooks across the continent.
