Bunny Chow

Cultural background

Bunny Chow is a beloved South African street-food classic, usually made with a hollowed loaf filled with fragrant curry and served in a single, satisfying portion.

70 minPrep
70 minCook
4Servings
EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

Instructions

Step 1

Fry onion, garlic and ginger with masala.

Step 2

Add meat, tomato and potato; simmer until tender.

Step 3

Cut the loaf in half and hollow it out.

Step 4

Fill generously and serve with the bread lid.
