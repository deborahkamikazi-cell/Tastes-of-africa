Rwanda · East Africa

Isombe

Cultural background

Isombe is a beloved Rwandan dish made from cassava leaves, often served in family meals with a rich, creamy, earthy flavor from groundnuts and vegetables.

75 minPrep
75 minCook
4Servings
EasyDifficulty

Ingredients

Instructions

Step 1

Boil cassava leaves for 40 minutes to soften.

Step 2

Sauté onion and garlic, then add eggplant.

Step 3

Stir in leaves and peanut paste.

Step 4

Simmer until thick and creamy, seasoning to taste.
