Jollof Rice

Cultural background

Jollof rice is a celebratory dish beloved across West Africa, known for its smoky depth, rich tomato base, and crowd-pleasing aroma. It is often served during festivals, weddings, and family gatherings.

60 minPrep
60 minCook
4Servings
MediumDifficulty

Ingredients

Instructions

Step 1

Blend tomatoes, peppers, and onion into a smooth base.

Step 2

Fry the base in oil until it darkens and the oil rises, about 25 minutes.

Step 3

Add stock and spices, then stir in washed rice.

Step 4

Cover tightly and steam on low heat until tender. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
