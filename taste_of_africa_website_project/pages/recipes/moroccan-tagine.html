Morocco · North Africa

Moroccan Tagine

Cultural background

Moroccan tagine is a slow-cooked braise known for fragrant spices, sweet-savory balance, and the way it turns a simple evening meal into an occasion.

120 minPrep
120 minCook
4Servings
MediumDifficulty

Ingredients

Instructions

Step 1

Marinate lamb with spices for an hour.

Step 2

Brown with onions in the tagine base.

Step 3

Add water, cover, and braise on low heat.

Step 4

Add apricots and lemon for the final 20 minutes.
