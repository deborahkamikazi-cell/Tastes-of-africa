West Africa

West Africa Cuisine

Bold, smoky, peppery flavors built on slow-cooked sauces and one-pot rice. The cuisine is shaped by a balance of heat, depth, and communal cooking.

Ingredients: Scotch bonnet, palm oil, egusi, thyme, grains of selim

Techniques: Slow stewing, pounding, open-fire smoking

East Africa

East Africa Cuisine

Fermented breads, aromatic spice blends, and hearty stews shared from one plate. The region celebrates communal texture, spice, and comforting fresh flavors.

Ingredients: Teff, berbere, cassava leaves, plantain, cardamom

Techniques: Fermenting, griddling, slow simmering

North Africa

North Africa Cuisine

Fragrant braises, couscous and the sweet-and-savory harmony of Mediterranean trade. North African dishes pair warm spice with citrus, dried fruit, and rich slow cooking.

Ingredients: Ras el hanout, saffron, preserved lemon, harissa, dates

Techniques: Tagine braising, steaming couscous, charcoal grilling
